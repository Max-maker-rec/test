How it works

Live under your brand
in two weeks.

We do the setup. Your team learns the platform in one afternoon and starts selling.

  • 01
    We brand the platform
    Your domain (for example jobs.youragency.com), logo, colours, email templates and ad templates. We connect your ATS and your ad accounts.

    Your team goes live

    Sell it to your clients
