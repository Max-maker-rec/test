How it works

Three steps.
Interview-ready candidates.

We handle everything between "we need someone" and "they're in your calendar." You just interview and decide.

  • 01
    Tell us what you need
    Fill in a 2-minute brief: role type, city, volume, and start date. That's it. No integration setup, no ad account, no platform to learn.
  • 02
    We run everything
    Our team launches targeted social campaigns across LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. AI creates the ads, screens the applicants, and filters out unqualified leads — all without you lifting a finger.
  • 03
    Hires land in your ATS
    Interview-ready candidates sync directly into your ATS or inbox. You review, you choose, you hire. We invoice only for the hires that happen.
