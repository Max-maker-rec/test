How it works

From job opening
to social ad in minutes

No agency, no guesswork. RYCS reads your ATS, crafts the creatives, and launches campaigns across every channel — then optimises them automatically.

  • 01
    Connect your ATS or job feed
    Works with Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Bullhorn, SmartRecruiters and 60+ others via native integrations or XML feed.
  • 02
    AI builds your ad creatives
    Our engine generates scroll-stopping copy and visuals, tailored for each platform's format and audience behaviour.
  • 03
    Launch across all channels
    One click distributes your ads to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and more — with budgets allocated by predicted ROI.
  • 04
    Optimise and close the role
    Real-time analytics show you cost-per-apply, quality scores and application velocity — with automated budget shifts to top performers.
