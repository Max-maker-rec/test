How it works

Your vacancy in.
Screened candidates out.

Your client relationship stays yours. Candidates know your agency's name, not ours.

  • 01
    Send us your open vacancies
    Forward a job order, connect your job feed, or link your ATS (Bullhorn, Carerix, Mysolution, Recruitee and more). Volume or single roles both work.
  • 02
    We run campaigns in your name
    White-label ads on LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, with your logo and your tone. We screen every applicant against your criteria: availability, location, salary and must-haves.
  • 03
    You present, place and invoice
    Candidates land in your ATS with screening notes. Your recruiter presents them to the client and closes. Your placement fee stays yours; we invoice our fixed fee only after the placement.
